# CTF Learning

A private self-study learning context for juniors already solving introductory CTF challenges, training across categories.

## Language

**Learner**:
A junior who already solves introductory CTF challenges and uses AI guidance to develop independent investigative skills.
_Avoid_: Complete beginner

**Practice challenge**:
A CTF exercise undertaken for learning, outside live competition and post-competition review.

**Tool proficiency**:
The ability to choose, use, and interpret a tool appropriately for the scenario, including recognizing what information to investigate next.
_Avoid_: Command memorization

**Guided investigation**:
Learner-operated investigation using the loop observation → learner interpretation → next hypothesis → experiment. Coaching escalates from conceptual hints to investigative directions, explained commands/scaffolds, and synthetic worked examples without supplying the challenge solution.
_Avoid_: Automatic solving

**Challenge reading**:
The learner's examination of a challenge's title and description for clues before choosing an investigative direction.

**Challenge write-up**:
A challenge-specific walkthrough or solution, including publicly available ones, that the coach must not supply to the learner.
_Avoid_: Foundational reference

**Foundational reference**:
General documentation that teaches a tool or concept without revealing a challenge-specific solution, including tool man pages and official documentation.

**Learning checkpoint**:
An optional local summary of observations, status-labeled hypotheses, learning progress, and the next experiment, saved only after learner approval of a preview. It supports continuity across sessions without including flags, credentials, raw transcripts, or complete solutions.
_Avoid_: Required write-up, mentor report

**Private self-study**:
Learning without required mentor submissions, curated write-ups, or transcript collection.
